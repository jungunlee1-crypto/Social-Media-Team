# Influencer Seeding

Run by `influencer-scout`. Read `social/references/social-guardrails.md` §3 first. Goal from ClickUp: a curated list of 20 amateur Philippine athletes and coaches with outreach templates. Depends on Provex's own social profiles being live and verified.

## Scoring rubric (100 points)

| Criterion | Points | How to judge (public data, with links) |
|---|---|---|
| Sport fit | 25 | Posts mostly about a sport where taping is common: basketball, volleyball, running, football, combat sports, CrossFit |
| Audience fit | 20 | Philippine audience, 18–40, comments from athletes |
| Engagement quality | 20 | Real comments and conversation; median views ≥ 10% of followers; no sudden follower jumps |
| Content quality | 15 | Clear, honest, well shot; already shows prep or routines |
| Brand safety | 15 | No hate, gambling, misinformation, medical claims, competing tape deals |
| Reliability | 5 | Posts at least weekly for 3 months |

Shortlist at 70+. Any brand-safety red flag is out, whatever the score.

## Tiers
- **Seed:** product only, no obligation to post. The default.
- **Paid:** only with the owner's approved fee and a written agreement. Not part of this skill's drafts.

## Outreach rules
- The owner sends every message from Provex's own account.
- Honest and personal: why them, what we'd send, no obligation to post, how to disclose if they do (#ad or the platform's paid-partnership label, as the platform requires).
- Never script results ("my knee pain is gone"). Suggest the format, not the words.
- Track replies in Drive, not the repo.

## Output
`SEEDING-LIST.md` (handles, platform, sport, score, evidence links, risks) and `OUTREACH-<batch>.md` from `templates/influencer-outreach.md`.
