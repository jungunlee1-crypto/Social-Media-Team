# Platform Playbooks

**As of:** 25 Oct 2026. **Status:** skeleton. Facts marked *Verify* have not been checked against an official source yet; the first `/social trends` run and every `/social review` should fill them in with a link and date. Ad specs (sizes, safe zones) belong to Paid Ads' source registry; link to it, don't copy it.

Audience (from the approved bios, 11 Oct 2026): pro and amateur athletes in the Philippines, 18–40. Voice: purposeful, not loud. The athlete is the hero.

## TikTok
- **Role:** discovery and education. Taping tutorials, prep routines, "tape it right" myths, court and field culture.
- **Formats to test:** 15–45 s tutorials, POV pre-game prep, duets/stitches of sports clips (no injury framing), series ("Taping 101, part 3").
- **Research tool:** TikTok Business connector (trending list, video list, hashtag tools).
- **Bio:** 80 characters (approved bio uses 66).
- *Verify:* current caption length limit, AI-content label rule, commercial music library rules for business accounts.

## Instagram
- **Role:** brand home and saves. Carousels for how-tos, Reels for reach, Stories for community.
- **Formats to test:** 4–8 slide how-to carousels, 15–30 s Reels, Story polls and quizzes.
- **Research tool:** Apify Instagram actors (public profiles, hashtags, Reels).
- **Bio:** 150 characters (approved bio uses 132).
- *Verify:* Reels length limits, paid-partnership label rules, AI label rule.

## Facebook
- **Role:** the biggest reach in the Philippines, community groups (running clubs, barangay leagues, PT and sports-medicine groups), older amateur athletes, Shopee/Lazada traffic.
- **Formats to test:** Reels cross-posted from Instagram, native video, photo posts with longer captions, Taglish posts, Page Q&A.
- **Community groups:** never post into a group from the team. The calendar marks which posts are "OK for FB community groups?" so the owner can share them personally, following each group's rules.
- **Research tool:** Apify Facebook actors (public Pages and public posts only).
- **Page intro:** 101 characters (approved intro uses 98).
- *Verify:* group posting rules for Pages, branded content tool rules, AI label rule.

## YouTube
- **Role:** search and depth. Longer taping guides people look up ("how to tape an ankle for basketball"), Shorts for reach.
- **Formats to test:** 3–8 min guides per body part, Shorts cut from TikTok tutorials.
- **Research tool:** TubeLab connector (outliers, channel and video data, transcripts). Only TubeLab's search tools cost credits.
- *Verify:* Shorts length limit, altered-or-synthetic content disclosure rule.

## X
- **Role:** smallest channel for this audience. Sports conversation, live game moments (PBA, UAAP, NCAA, marathons), quick tips, customer questions.
- **Formats to test:** short tip posts with one image, threads that summarise a tutorial, replies drafted for live sports moments (the owner posts).
- **Research tool:** Apify X actors (public posts and search).
- **Effort cap:** proposed at 1 post a day until `/social review` shows it earns more.
- *Verify:* post length limit for the account's tier, AI and synthetic media policy.

## Cross-posting rules
- Make it once, adapt per platform: native captions, native text styles, no other platform's watermark.
- Keep the same post ID across platforms in the calendar so results can be compared.
