# Content Pillars

**As of:** 25 Oct 2026. **Status:** approved by the owner (Angela Lee), 25 Oct 2026.

| Pillar | Share (proposed) | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Education** | 40% | How to tape and prepare, done right | Finger buddy-tape for basketball, ankle tape for volleyball, rigid vs. kinesiology, tape myths |
| **Community** | 25% | Filipino athletes and their culture | Barangay court stories, running-club shout-outs (with permission), "your pre-game ritual?" polls |
| **Product** | 20% | What Provex makes, plainly | Widths and colours, how to read the label, what's in the box, CE marking |
| **Athlete stories** | 15% | Preparation, grit, performance; the athlete is the hero | Synthetic brand characters from Creative, or real athletes only with a signed release |

## Rules
- Education never becomes medical advice (guardrails §2). Show prep and support, not recovery from injury.
- Product posts use only Offer Sheet claims.
- Athlete stories with real people need a signed release in Drive before planning starts.
- Shares are a starting point. `/social review` proposes changes after four weeks of results.
