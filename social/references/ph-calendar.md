## Rules
- Never tie a post to a tragedy, typhoon or disaster. During a major storm or national emergency, `content-planner` proposes pausing the calendar.
- League, team and event names are trademarks. Talk about the moment ("finals week"), not the brand, unless the owner has rights (Creative guardrails §5).
