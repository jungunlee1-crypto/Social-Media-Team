## The study (owner brief, 25 Oct 2026)

For each brand, find its top 10 posts per platform by engagement and work out why they performed: format (video vs. image, short vs. long), colours, caption, hashtags, headline, hook, who's in it, audio, posting time, or anything else. Record each post in the sheet's Top Posts tab, the cross-brand patterns in the Patterns tab, and report the findings to the owner.
